## Ben has been the luckiest contender

Ben is 6-4 with a losing all-play record and more points against than for. He has been a below-median scorer who keeps drawing the right opponent at the right time. He is about to be 7-4 on a 116-point week, which is above his season average, so the luck cuts both ways this week. But the full picture is a team built to finish around .500 sitting in a playoff spot.

## Jesse has been the unluckiest

Jesse leads the league in points (1,248.68) and the all-play table at 65-25. He has finished top three six times in ten weeks. He is 6-4 and probably about to be 6-5. No one has scored more and gotten less for it.

## Sammy found a second gear

The bigger story is the trend. Sammy averaged 111.7 points over Weeks 1-6. Over Weeks 7-10 he averaged **136.6**, the best stretch in the league by a lot. Nobody else gained more than 12.2 a week over the same split. He is 7-3 and his record is honest.

## Nick is going the other way

Nick averaged 101.9 over the first six weeks and 75.6 over the last four, a drop of 26.3 per week. Six bottom-three finishes in ten weeks. The playoff odds are already near zero; this is about the draft order now.
