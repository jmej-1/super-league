Week 12 is the last week to trade. Sleeper closes the window when the week's final game ends, which this week is Panthers at Buccaneers on Monday night, November 30. Trades also sit in a one-day review, so anything agreed on Monday afternoon is cutting it close. After that, every roster is what it is for the playoffs.

Here's the board.

## The buyers: three teams, one coin flip

| Team | Record | Playoffs | Biggest hole |
|---|---|---|---|
| {t:3} | 5-6 | 73.8% | QB (-0.13 WAR) |
| {t:10} | 5-6 | 65.3% | QB (-0.29 WAR) |
| {t:6} | 5-6 | 50.6% | TE (-0.25 WAR), QB (-0.11) |

**Jason is the most desperate team in the league.** He's a pure coin flip, and his Week 12 game against {t:2} swings his playoff odds by 41 points. {p:9221} has carried him to +2.45 WAR on his own, but {p:7523} and {p:7600} have given him less than nothing. If anyone needs to make a call before kickoff, it's Jason.

**Thomas** already went all in once, getting {p:7564} from Sammy. His quarterback spot is still the weakest of any bubble team.

**Zack** is in the best shape of the three, but he's gotten negative value from the quarterback spot all year too. That's a theme.

## The sellers: three teams with nothing left to play for

- **{t:8} (0.2% playoffs)** has the best trade chip in the league for this exact market: {p:3294}, +0.45 WAR, coming off 35.9 points in a loss. Every bubble team needs a quarterback, and Beck has the one worth having. He also has {p:8151} (+1.57) and {p:8228} (+0.89) at running back.
- **{t:4} (4%)** has {p:8138} (+1.45) and {p:8150} (+1.00), two starting running backs on a team that's playing for draft position, plus {p:6794}.
- **{t:9} (9%)** is still technically alive, but the wide receiver room is a contender's dream: {p:6786} (+1.55), {p:6801} (+0.99) and {p:8144} (+0.69). Hamish could sell one and still field a lineup.

## The contenders: shopping for the bye

The top four are all at 97% or better to make the playoffs, so for them this is about the two byes.

- **{t:7}** leads the bye race at 75%, and has the worst quarterback production of any contender (-0.42 WAR). He's also played three of the last four weeks with an empty slot. A tight end and a kicker off waivers cost nothing.
- **{t:1}** just lost 25 points of bye odds on one walk-off. Sammy has been part of 8 trades this season, double anyone else, so don't expect him to sit still.
- **{t:2}** has the most points in the league and hasn't made a single trade all year. He plays Jason this week.

## The Walker question

{p:8151} has missed two straight games with a sore groin. The Chiefs have called it short-term, and the reporting has pointed at Thanksgiving night in Buffalo as the real target. Meanwhile {p:13337} has run with the job, including 171 yards from scrimmage and two scores against Atlanta in Week 10. For a buyer, Walker is the classic deadline gamble: a top back from a team that's out of it, with a health question that may already be answered by Thursday.

The window closes Monday night. After that, the only moves left are waivers.
